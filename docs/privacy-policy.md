# Merge Fruit — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2, 2026_

Merge Fruit ("the app") is a simple merge-puzzle game. This page explains
what data it collects and how it's used.

## What we collect

- **Account information**: the email address and password you provide when
  you register or log in. Your password is never stored in plain text — the
  backend stores only a salted hash of it, and cannot recover the original
  password.
- **Gameplay data**: your scores, submitted to power the in-app leaderboard.

You can also play as a guest without an account, in which case no account
information is collected and scores are not submitted.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or
any data beyond what's listed above. The app requests no permissions other
than network (internet) access.

## How it's used

Account data is used solely to let you log in and to associate your scores
with your account. Score data is used solely to display leaderboards inside
the app.

## Who we share it with

Your data is sent to the app's own backend server (`merge-fruit-api`) and is
not shared with any third party. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no
analytics/tracking SDKs, and no third-party data brokers.

## Data retention and deletion

Your account and score data are kept for as long as your account exists. To
request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the
email address below.

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
